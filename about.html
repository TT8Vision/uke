Who we are

UK Emporium is Cape Town's dedicated importer of authentic British and European goods. Since 2004, we've been sourcing directly from UK manufacturers and distributing nationally across South Africa — bringing the real thing to shelves that too often settle for substitutes.

We supply a wide range of outlets: national supermarket chains, independent delis, forecourts, online retailers and individual customers. Regardless of size, every client receives the same commitment from our team.

Our range spans 13 categories — cleaning products, confectionery, hot and cold drinks, groceries, personal care, snacks and more — covering brands like Astonish, Belvoir, Kent, Duerrs, Tracklements and Encona.
